About me

Hi, I'm Ivonnia.

I hold a degree in Psychology and a Master's in Child and Adolescent Clinical Psychology. I am currently pursuing a PhD in Psychology at the Pontificia Universidad Católica Argentina (UCA), with a joint UCA-CONICET doctoral fellowship.

My doctoral research is titled “The moderating effect of digital competencies on the impact of social media use on body image.” I study how social media shapes the way we perceive and relate to our bodies, and the role digital competencies play in that relationship.

I see therapy as a unique and personal process. That's why each therapeutic space is built individually, taking into account each person's history, needs and goals, in an environment of trust, listening and no judgment.

Approach

How do I work?

I work from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), using evidence-based treatments. Alongside my clinical practice I do research, which lets me bring the most up-to-date knowledge about what works into therapy.

i

Body and context

We think, feel and live through our bodies. That's why I work with your history, your relationships and the environments you inhabit, digital ones included.

ii

Listening and trust

A confidential and respectful space where you can speak freely and at your own pace.

iii

Evidence and clear goals

Scientifically supported treatments, with goals we set and review together.

Services

How can I help?

Anxiety and depression

Tools to understand and manage worry, fear, sadness and low energy.

Stress and overload

For when demands outweigh your resources and it's hard to set limits or rest.

Overcontrol and rigidity

Grief and loss

Support through the loss of a loved one, a relationship, a project or a stage of life.

Self-esteem

Strengthening your relationship with yourself, working on self-criticism and building a kinder view of who you are.

Body image and eating

Concerns about your body, food or weight, including eating disorders.
